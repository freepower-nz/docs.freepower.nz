---
title: "Smart Actions"
---

**Smart Actions** are the recommended entry point for most consumers and developers.

These endpoints are designed to simplify complex workflows into a single, easy-to-use call. They combine multiple internal steps and return only the most relevant information — so you don’t have to deal with unnecessary detail or configuration.

Use Smart Actions when you want:

- A clear answer without navigating multiple endpoints
- Just enough data to make a decision (not everything under the hood)
- A smooth experience for AI assistants, chatbots, or front-end apps

These are the default choice for everyday use cases.
